# Verification boundary for version 1.1.0

The universal result is the written manuscript proof. Exact computations are supporting finite checks, not a proof of all distributions or all measurable disclosures.

The main replay checks seven stable recorded result sets and byte identity of all 9,236 failure witnesses. Its 6,560 nonzero count vectors cover 255 supports but only 6,305 distinct normalized distributions. Revision checks cover 12,800 nonconstant orientations, 32 projective comparisons for the strict separator, and five input/witness rejection controls. Explicit exceptions remain active under -O and the runner propagates optimisation to subprocesses.

Publication runs are executed again from the final frozen archive, normally and under optimized Python. Their receipt is retained by the publication process and CI; absence of a receipt must not be interpreted as success. Earlier input results/audits are historical records. All checks are producer-coordinated internal evidence. No independent external reproduction, specialist peer review or formal verification is established.
